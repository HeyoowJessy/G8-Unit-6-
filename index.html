4. Lesson Content

What is SDG?

SDG means Sustainable Development Goal. The United Nations made 17 global goals to help people, protect the planet, and make the world better by 2030.

The 17 SDGs

The 3Rs

Reduce

  • Bring an eco bag when shopping.
  • Say no to extra straw or package waste.

Reuse

  • Reuse a basket for classroom materials.
  • Use washable spoon and chopsticks again.

Recycle

  • Recycle paper and newspaper.
  • Sort plastic and metal correctly.
